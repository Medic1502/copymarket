Features

Everything you need to copy smart

💳

Flexible deposits

Fund your account by card via Moonpay, or send USDC directly from any external wallet or exchange — MetaMask, Coinbase, Binance, whatever you use.

🔒

Proportional scaling

If a trader bets 10% of their portfolio, you bet 10% of yours — regardless of the difference in size.

🛡️

Automatic safety limits

Set a max per trade and a daily loss limit. Copy trading pauses automatically when limits are hit.

📊

Live dashboard

Track every copied trade in real time. P&L, win rate, open positions — all in one place.

Trade notifications

Get a push notification for every trade executed on your behalf. You always know what's happening.

Liquidity filter

Trades with too little volume are automatically skipped — you never enter a market you can't exit.

Pause in one click

Pause all copy trading instantly. Your money stays in open positions — no new trades are placed.

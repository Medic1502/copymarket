Welcome to Jonin CT!
Follow these steps to start copying trades
1
Export your wallet
Go to the Wallet tab → click Export private key → copy your 12-word seed phrase.
2
Import into MetaMask
Open MetaMask → Add wallet → Import Wallet → paste your 12-word seed phrase.
3
Register on Polymarket
Go to polymarket.com → register with your new MetaMask wallet → accept Terms of Service. Then deposit USDC to your Polymarket profile.
4
Add a trader & start earning
Click + Add trader and hit Resume. The bot will copy trades automatically 24/7.
